Reflections

What I expect from AI Engineering October 14, 2026

As a first-year student, I joined AI Engineering to learn how intelligent systems are actually built, not just how they are used. I hope to become comfortable with programming, math, and working with data. What scares me a little is how fast the field changes, and I worry about falling behind. What has surprised me in these first weeks is how much of AI depends on basics like clear thinking, good questions, and patience with debugging.

Building my first portfolio website October 14, 2026

Creating this site for AIE100 taught me more than I expected. I learned that a website is just files stored on a server, and that a small mistake, like a wrongly named file, can stop everything from working. I also learned how to use GitHub to publish and update my work. Next time, I will plan my content before I start, and I will keep updating this portfolio as I finish new projects.

Learning to question AI answers October 12, 2026

While using AI tools for studying, I noticed that they can give confident answers that are partly wrong. This taught me that using AI well means checking its output against reliable sources and my own understanding. From now on, I will treat AI as a helpful assistant, not as the final authority, and I will keep practicing how to verify what it tells me.
